Ledger

Version 1.1 · runs entirely on your phone

Ledger estimates how the purchasing power of an amount changes between two years, for a selected country, using annual inflation (CPI) rates.

Actual figures (2000–2026) are representative sample data for demonstration — not an official economic source. Years 2027–2031 are a simple projection based on each country's recent average rate, clearly marked as estimates throughout the app.

Your calculation history is saved only on this device, in this browser.
